# The Kash+ banner reads the same on a phone as on the desk

The phone showed a flat export of the Kash+ banner with its lettering baked
into the image, so its words were a different face from the desktop banner and
from every other heading in the app, and they stayed in English in every
locale.

The phone now shows the same banner the desk does. Its words are real text in
the app's own faces, the headline scales to the column it is given, and the
exported PNG is gone.
