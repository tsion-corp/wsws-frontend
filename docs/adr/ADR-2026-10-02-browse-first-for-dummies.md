# ADR for Dummies: Look around first, sign in when you want to play

- **Status:** Accepted 2026-10-02 by the maintainer
- **Date:** 2026-10-02

## The Idea

Right now you have to sign in before you can see anything in Ark. We want
anyone to be able to look around first: browse the markets, watch the
games, open a link a friend sent. You only need to sign in when you want to
_do_ something with money: play, buy, sell, bet, deposit or withdraw.

## What the Person Sees

1. A friend sends a link to their private Last Man game. You open it without
   an account and see the game running, the pot and the clock.
2. You press Play. A message pops up: "Sign in to play", with a Sign in
   button.
3. You sign in in a small window, without leaving the page, and you're back
   at the game, ready to play.
4. Anywhere in the app, signed out, the bottom of the side menu shows a
   **Sign in** button instead of your name and picture.
5. Your balance reads $0.00 with "Sign in to see your balance" underneath,
   so nobody thinks their money is gone.

## Also Changing

- The "pick your interests" page after sign-up goes away. The home page is
  laid out the same for everyone: your balance, then **Arkade**, Join the
  Conversation, Prediction, Real assets, Memecoins, then the rest. The side menu stays as it is.

## What Stays Signed-In Only

- Upgrading an old account, because that is something you do to your
  account.
- Your own things (balance, history, referrals, your Ark ID) simply show as
  empty until you sign in.

## What Does Not Change

- Nothing about how money moves once you are signed in.
- The servers still refuse any money action without a sign-in, so nothing
  becomes less safe.

## Added 2026-10-03: Add funds right where you need it

If a game, a market or a purchase tells you your balance is too low, an
"Add funds" button now sits right next to that message. Tap it, top up, and
carry on where you were. This matters most for someone who just followed a
shared link: they sign in, fund and play without leaving the page.
